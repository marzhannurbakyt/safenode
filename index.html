Safety for the moments when people cannot ask for help.

SafeNode is developing hardware-based technology for emergency situations where a person may be unable to call, reach a phone, or manually initiate an alert.

Explore the technology Current stage
01 / The Problem

What happens when a person cannot call for help?

Many emergency systems assume that a person can recognize the situation, reach a device, and actively trigger an alert. Some emergencies do not allow this.

01

Unable to react

A person may be unconscious, immobilized, injured, or otherwise unable to interact with a phone or SOS button.

02

Working alone

Lone workers and people in isolated environments may face emergencies without immediate assistance nearby.

03

Communication gaps

Conventional emergency workflows can depend on a single device, network, or person initiating the request for help.

02 / Our Approach

From passive alerts to intelligent physical safety.

SafeNode explores how sensing, embedded intelligence and resilient communication can work together to support emergency response.

01
Sense Capture relevant physical and environmental signals.
02
Interpret Identify potentially critical events and reduce false alarms.
03
Connect Explore reliable ways to transmit an emergency signal.
04
Respond Provide appropriate local or remote intervention.
03 / Technology

Hardware-first. Built for the physical world.

The final architecture is being validated through technical experimentation. Potential building blocks include physical sensing, embedded processing and resilient communication.

Physical sensing

Motion and other contextual signals that may help characterize abnormal events.

Embedded intelligence

Local processing and event detection designed to operate close to the physical environment.

Resilient communication

Investigation of device-to-device and other communication approaches for emergency signaling.

The architecture is not fixed yet.

We are deliberately validating the technical approach before committing to a final device, communication protocol, or form factor. The goal is to build the simplest system that can demonstrate reliable real-world value.

04 / Applications

One technology. Multiple safety environments.

We are starting with high-value emergency scenarios and will validate where the technology creates the strongest practical benefit.

Initial exploration

Personal Safety

Emergency support for people who may be alone or unable to manually request assistance.

Expansion

Lone Workers

Safety infrastructure for employees working without immediate access to colleagues.

Future

Construction & Logistics

Potential applications in environments where physical work, isolation and emergency response are critical.

Future

Industrial Safety

Exploring how intelligent physical safety systems could support industrial personnel and existing safety infrastructure.

05 / Current Stage

Research first. Evidence before scale.

SafeNode is an early-stage project. We are validating the problem, technical feasibility, communication architecture and appropriate physical form factor before committing to a final product.

Current focus: Technical PoC + real-world validation

The next step is to build small, testable prototypes, evaluate emergency scenarios under controlled conditions, and use the resulting evidence to define the product architecture.

06 / Vision
Make emergency response less dependent on human reaction.
